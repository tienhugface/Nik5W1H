Privacy Policy for Nik5W1H

Last updated: September 2026

Nik5W1H is designed to help users record, organize, search, and retrieve information about personal belongings, people, documents, procedures, and other memories.

Your Data

Information entered into Nik5W1H may include text, notes, dates, locations, photos, scanned documents, files, and other information that you choose to store.

Nik5W1H does not operate an external developer-controlled server for storing your personal records.

Depending on the storage option you select, your information is stored either locally on your device or in your personal iCloud account using Apple’s services.

Artificial Intelligence Features

On supported devices, Nik5W1H can use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models and Apple Intelligence to provide AI-assisted features, including:

* organizing natural-language descriptions into What, Where, When, Who, Why, and How information;
* assisting with memory entry; and
* answering questions based on information stored in the app.

When Apple’s on-device AI is used, the information processed by these features is handled by Apple’s on-device model.

Nik5W1H does not send this information to the developer for AI processing.

AI-generated information may be incomplete or inaccurate. Users should review AI-generated or AI-organized information before relying on or saving it.

Devices Without Apple Intelligence

On devices that do not support Apple Intelligence, on-device AI features may be unavailable.

Nik5W1H does not send users’ personal memory information to a third-party AI service as a substitute for unavailable Apple Intelligence.

Speech Recognition

Nik5W1H may use Apple’s speech recognition technologies when you choose to enter information using your voice.

Speech recognition availability and processing depend on the device, language, and Apple’s operating system services.

Nik5W1H does not operate its own speech-recognition server.

Location

Nik5W1H accesses location information only when you choose to use a location-related feature and grant the required permission.

Location information may be stored as part of a memory item at your request.

Nik5W1H does not continuously track your location.

Photos, Camera, Documents and Files

Nik5W1H may request access to the camera, photo library, document scanner, or files when you choose to attach this information to a memory.

These permissions are used only to provide the feature you requested.

iCloud

If you choose iCloud storage, information is stored and synchronized using Apple’s iCloud services and is subject to Apple’s privacy and security practices.

You may instead choose to keep your Nik5W1H data in local device storage where supported by the app.

Data Collection

Nik5W1H does not use your personal memory information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or sale to third parties.

The developer does not sell your personal information.

Permissions

Nik5W1H may request access to features such as:

* Microphone
* Speech Recognition
* Camera
* Photo Library
* Location
* Files and Documents
* iCloud

Access is requested only when required for app functionality and is subject to the permissions you grant through the operating system.

Your Control

You control the information you create and store in Nik5W1H.

You can edit or delete individual records and use the app’s backup and restore features where available.

Your ability to manage information stored in iCloud is also subject to Apple’s iCloud services and settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Nik5W1H features or privacy practices change. The latest version will show the date of the most recent update.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Nik5W1H’s handling of information, please contact the developer using the support contact provided on the Nik5W1H App Store listing.
